Mini Biography: Dr. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Abdul Kalam was a famous Indian scientist, teacher, and former President of India. He inspired millions of young people with his knowledge and positive attitude.

Early Life

Abdul Kalam was born in India, in 1931. He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams.

Important Achievement

Dr. Kalam made many important contributions to India's space and missile programs. He is known for his important role in developing India's missile technology and was often called the Missile Man of India. His dedication made him an inspiration to many people. He later became the President of India.

Some people wrongly think that Dr. Kalam was only a politician, but he was mainly known as a politician but he was also a brilliant scientist and teacher.
